Travel & Stay Info

⚜

Helpful guidelines for our out-of-town guests travelling to Dirba, Punjab.

By Air

Chandigarh Int'l Airport (IXC): ~115 km
Delhi IGI Airport (DEL): ~240 km
Taxis and chauffeur cars are readily available via NH-52.

🚆

By Train

Sangrur Railway Station (SAG): ~25 km
Patiala Railway Station (PTA): ~60 km
Direct connectivity from New Delhi, Ludhiana & Ambala.

Valet Parking

Spacious, dedicated and secure parking for 400+ vehicles is available inside The Grand Vilas premises with valet assistance.

Arrangements have been reserved for outstation guests. Please contact our hospitality desk upon arrival in Dirba for suite allocations.
